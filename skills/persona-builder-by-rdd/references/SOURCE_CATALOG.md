# Frozen public source catalog

Access date for this release: 2026-10-03.

This catalog documents the public sources used to design the reusable research and communication methods in `persona-builder-by-rdd`. It is not the future avatar-specific source catalog; every generated child must build its own corpus for its own niche, geography, language, and date.

## S001 - Strategyzer Value Proposition Canvas
URL: https://www.strategyzer.com/library/the-value-proposition-canvas
Publisher: Strategyzer
Source class: specialist framework publisher
Use: one customer segment, jobs, pains, gains, and evidence testing.
Limit: framework structure does not prove market prevalence.

## S002 - Strategyzer value proposition overview
URL: https://www.strategyzer.com/value-proposition
Publisher: Strategyzer
Source class: specialist framework publisher
Use: prioritizing customer-relevant jobs, pains, and gains, including functional, social, and emotional dimensions.
Limit: company-authored explanation of its own framework.

## S003 - Know Your Customers' Jobs to Be Done
URL: https://hbr.org/2016/09/know-your-customers-jobs-to-be-done
Authors: Clayton M. Christensen, Taddy Hall, Karen Dillon, David S. Duncan
Publisher: Harvard Business Review
Publication: September 2016
Source class: management research/practitioner article
Use: understand customer choices through the progress or job sought in context rather than static profile alone.
Limit: apply as a research lens, not a deterministic classification system.

## S004 - EAST methodology
URL: https://www.bi.team/east-tool/methodology/
Publisher: Behavioural Insights Team
Source class: applied behavioral science organization
Use: Easy, Attractive, Social, Timely principles for practical behavioral design.
Limit: BIT states that EAST is not a comprehensive catalog of behavioral effects.

## S005 - EAST publication overview
URL: https://www.bi.team/publications/east-four-simple-ways-to-apply-behavioural-insights/
Publisher: Behavioural Insights Team
Source class: applied behavioral science organization
Use: history and updated status of EAST, including the 2024 revision.
Limit: practitioner framework; effectiveness depends on context and evaluation.

## S006 - APA interview with Robert Cialdini
URL: https://www.apa.org/news/podcasts/speaking-of-psychology/persuasion
Publisher: American Psychological Association
Source class: professional psychology organization/interview
Use: persuasion as a studied area; ethical, nonmanipulative application of influence principles.
Limit: interview format, not a systematic review of every persuasion technique.

## S007 - Cialdini principles overview
URL: https://www.influenceatwork.com/7-principles-of-persuasion/
Publisher: Influence at Work
Source class: author/consulting organization
Use: public description of reciprocity, liking, scarcity, social proof, authority, unity, and consistency with an ethical-use framing.
Limit: commercial site connected to the framework author.

## S008 - CDC Clear Communication Index: description and examples
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/tool/description-examples-parta.html
Publisher: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
Source class: public health agency
Use: main message, call to action, familiar language, active voice, information hierarchy, headings, lists, state of knowledge.
Limit: developed for public health communication; adapt general design principles carefully outside health.

## S009 - CDC Clear Communication Index FAQ
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/tool/faq.html
Publisher: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
Source class: public health agency
Use: research-based communication assessment areas including message, plain language, information design, behavioral recommendations, numbers, and risk.
Limit: the Index does not replace direct audience research.

## S010 - CDC Clear Communication Index: how to use
URL: https://www.cdc.gov/ccindex/tool/how-to-use.html
Publisher: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention
Source class: public health agency
Use: put main message early, write for the primary audience, use chunks and headings, and make actions explicit.
Limit: channel and domain context still matter.

## S011 - NN/g Concise, Scannable, and Objective
URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/concise-scannable-and-objective-how-to-write-for-the-web/
Authors: John Morkes, Jakob Nielsen
Publisher: Nielsen Norman Group
Publication: 1997-01-01
Source class: usability research
Use: evidence for concise, scannable, objective web writing and reduced promotional hype.
Limit: older study; do not treat its measured percentages as current universal effects.

## S012 - NN/g Applying Writing Guidelines to Web Pages
URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/applying-writing-guidelines-web-pages/
Authors: John Morkes, Jakob Nielsen
Publisher: Nielsen Norman Group
Publication: 1998-01-06
Source class: usability research
Use: practical application of concise, scannable, objective writing.
Limit: older web context.

## S013 - Erico Rocha: O que sao lancamentos
URL: https://ericorocha.com.br/2020/08/04/o-que-sao-lancamentos/
Author: Erico Rocha
Source class: practitioner/provider public content
Use: public descriptions of launch types such as seed and internal launches and validation through an initial offer.
Limit: provider-authored; commercial performance claims are not independent evidence.

## S014 - Erico Rocha: antecipacao

Author: Erico Rocha
Source class: practitioner/provider public content
Use: public description of building attention before an offer.
Limit: practitioner claim; do not treat as a guaranteed behavioral effect.

## S015 - Erico Rocha: escassez

Author: Erico Rocha
