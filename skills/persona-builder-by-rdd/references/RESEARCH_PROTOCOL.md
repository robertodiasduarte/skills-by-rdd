# Research protocol for market personas

## Purpose
Build a useful market avatar from evidence instead of stereotypes. Research should explain what the avatar is trying to accomplish, what makes communication useful or difficult, what language appears natural, and what evidence supports each conclusion.

## Required research order
1. Define avatar, geography, language, and use cases.
2. Search primary, institutional, academic, professional, and specialized sources.
3. Search reputable market studies, surveys, interviews, reviews, and industry sources.
4. Search communities, forums, Reddit, YouTube, LinkedIn, and similar public conversations for qualitative language and recurring situations.
5. Compare source classes before converting observations into persona claims.
6. Record gaps and contradictions.

## Source hierarchy
Source quality is multidimensional. Consider authority, method, recency, directness, geography, population fit, and transparency.

Use these practical labels:
- `high_authority`: official, academic, professional association, documented survey, or recognized specialist source with relevant methodology.
- `market_evidence`: reputable research, interviews, reviews, or industry data useful for context and decisions.
- `qualitative_language`: public discussions and comments useful for vocabulary, objections, situations, and hypotheses.

A community thread can be excellent evidence of how a phrase is used and weak evidence of how common a belief is in the whole market.

## Evidence states
- `observed_evidence`: directly stated or measured in an identified source.
- `recurring_pattern`: similar signal across independent sources.
- `inference`: interpretation drawn from evidence.
- `hypothesis`: plausible but not sufficiently supported.

Never collapse these states.

## Practical confidence
- `strong`: direct and well-matched evidence, normally from high-authority sources or multiple independent sources.
- `moderate`: reasonable support, but some limits in population, date, geography, or method.
- `qualitative_signal`: useful for language, objections, or discovery, not a population-level claim.

## Search design
Use several query families:
- role responsibilities and workflow;
- decision criteria and purchasing context;
- recurring pain points and operational constraints;
- professional language and jargon;
- reviews of tools or services used by the avatar;
- interviews and podcasts with practitioners;
- communities and forums;
- regulation or technology changes that materially affect context;
- research on communication preferences when available.

Do not use only generic "persona" articles about the niche. Prefer evidence about real work, decisions, complaints, goals, and language.

## Capture format
For each source record:
- evidence ID;
- title;
- author or publisher;
- URL;
- publication date if known;
- access date;
- geography and population;
- source class;
- key finding in original words of the analyst, not copied prose;
- persona dimensions supported;
- limitations;
- evidence state and confidence.

## Language research
Capture recurring terms, metaphors, jargon, abbreviations, questions, and framing. Avoid long quotations. Record where the wording appeared and whether it is professional jargon, casual community language, marketing language, or an isolated expression.

Do not reproduce personally identifying usernames unless essential and authorized. Prefer generalized source references.

## Triangulation
For broad persona claims, seek support from at least two independent source classes when practical. Independence matters more than raw source count.

When evidence conflicts:
1. compare populations;
2. compare geography;
3. compare date;
4. compare source purpose and incentives;
5. compare methodology;
6. preserve the disagreement if it cannot be resolved.

## Segmentation gate
If evidence shows materially different jobs, authority, information needs, vocabulary, or decision criteria, propose separate avatars. Present at most three candidates and ask the user to select one for the current child skill.

## Update research
Child skills do not refresh themselves silently. New research occurs only at user request. Contradictory evidence becomes an update proposal, not an automatic persona change.
